Key Functions

Each department plays a critical role in India's financial ecosystem

Economic Affairs

Formulates economic policy, manages public debt, international financial relations, and oversees RBI coordination. Also handles currency and coinage.

Revenue

Administers direct and indirect taxes including Income Tax, GST, and Customs. Houses CBDT and CBIC for tax enforcement.
