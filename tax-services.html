Tax FAQs

Which ITR form should I use?

ITR-1 (Sahaj) for salaried individuals with income up to ₹50 lakhs. ITR-2 for individuals with capital gains. ITR-3 for business income. ITR-4 for presumptive taxation.

What is the deadline for filing ITR?

For individuals: July 31, 2026. For businesses requiring audit: October 31, 2026. Late filing attracts penalty up to ₹10,000.

How to link PAN with Aadhaar?

Visit incometax.gov.in, go to 'Link Aadhaar' under Quick Links, enter PAN and Aadhaar details, verify OTP, and submit. Linking is mandatory.
