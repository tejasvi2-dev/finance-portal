Union Budget 2026-27: Key Highlights and Allocations

May 10, 2026  |  5 min read

The Finance Minister presented the Union Budget 2026-27 with a total outlay of ₹48.13 Lakh Crore, marking India's largest-ever budget with a strong focus on infrastructure, healthcare, education, and digital transformation.

Infrastructure — ₹11.11 Lakh Crore

The government has allocated a record ₹11,11,111 Crore for infrastructure development, including new expressways, railway modernization, port development, and smart city initiatives. This represents a 12.3% increase over the previous year, signaling the government's commitment to building world-class infrastructure.

Tax Reforms

The new tax regime offers zero tax for income up to ₹7.5 Lakh, benefiting approximately 4 Crore taxpayers. The standard deduction has been increased to ₹75,000, and the surcharge on high-value incomes has been rationalized. Corporate tax for MSMEs has been reduced to 15% to boost manufacturing.

Digital Economy Push

With ₹10,000 Crore allocated for AI and emerging technologies, the budget underscores India's ambition to become a global digital economy leader. Key initiatives include expansion of the UPI infrastructure, digital rupee pilot in 50 more cities, and a new cybersecurity framework.

Social Welfare

Healthcare receives ₹89,155 Crore (up 22.1%), with expansion of Ayushman Bharat to cover 50 Crore additional beneficiaries. Education gets ₹1,20,628 Crore with focus on NEP implementation, 200 new Navodaya schools, and digital learning platforms for rural areas.
