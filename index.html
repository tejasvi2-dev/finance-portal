Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

What is GST and who needs to register?

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is an indirect tax applicable on supply of goods and services. Businesses with annual turnover exceeding ₹40 lakhs (₹20 lakhs for services) must register.

How can I file my Income Tax Return online?

Visit incometax.gov.in, log in with your PAN, select the appropriate ITR form, fill in your income details, and submit. You can also use our Tax Services portal for guided assistance.

How to apply for government financial schemes?

Visit our Schemes page, select the relevant scheme, check eligibility criteria, and apply online through the respective portal. Most schemes offer paperless digital applications.

Where can I download budget documents?

All budget documents, economic surveys, and annual reports are available in our Reports & Downloads section in PDF format for free download.
