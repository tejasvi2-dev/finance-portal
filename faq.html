GST
What is GST and who needs to register?

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is an indirect tax on supply of goods and services. Businesses with annual turnover exceeding ₹40 lakhs (₹20 lakhs for services) must register for GST.

How do I file GST returns?

Log in to the GST portal (gst.gov.in), navigate to Returns → File Returns, select the return type (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, etc.), fill in details, and submit. You can also use our Tax Services portal for assistance.

What are the GST tax slabs?

GST has four main slabs: 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%. Essential items like food grains are at 0%. Gold is at 3%. Luxury and sin goods attract additional cess above 28%.

Tax Filing
How can I file my Income Tax Return online?

Visit incometax.gov.in, log in with your PAN, select the appropriate ITR form, fill in income details, verify deductions, and submit. E-verify using Aadhaar OTP or net banking.

What is the deadline for filing ITR?

The deadline for individual taxpayers is July 31 of each assessment year. For FY 2025-26, it has been extended to August 31, 2026. Late filing attracts a penalty of up to ₹5,000.

Which ITR form should I use?

ITR-1 for salaried individuals (income up to ₹50 lakhs), ITR-2 for individuals with capital gains, ITR-3 for business income, ITR-4 for presumptive taxation scheme. Check the Income Tax website for detailed eligibility.

Government Schemes
How to apply for government financial schemes?

Visit our Schemes page, select the relevant scheme, check eligibility, and click "Apply Now" to fill the online application form.

Am I eligible for MUDRA Yojana?

MUDRA loans are available for micro/small businesses, artisans, street vendors, and individual entrepreneurs. Loans up to ₹10 Lakh are provided without collateral under three categories: Shishu (up to ₹50K), Kishore (₹50K–5L), and Tarun (₹5L–10L).

How does PM Kisan Samman Nidhi work?

Eligible farmer families receive ₹6,000 per year in three equal installments of ₹2,000 each, directly transferred to their bank accounts. Register through PM-Kisan portal or your nearest Common Service Center.
