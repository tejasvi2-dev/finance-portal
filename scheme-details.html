About the Scheme

Startup India is a flagship initiative launched to build a strong ecosystem for nurturing innovation and startups in India. The initiative aims to drive sustainable economic growth and generate large-scale employment opportunities.

Key Benefits
  • Tax exemptions for eligible startups for 3 consecutive years
  • Self-certification compliance for 6 labour laws and 3 environmental laws
  • Fast-track patent examination at lower costs
  • Access to ₹10,000 Crore Fund of Funds
  • Easy winding up of company within 90 days
  • Government procurement relaxation
Eligibility
  • Entity registered as Private Ltd, Partnership, or LLP
  • Incorporated for less than 10 years
  • Annual turnover not exceeding ₹100 Crore
  • Working towards innovation or scalable business model
  • Not formed by splitting/restructuring existing business
How to Apply
  1. Register on the Startup India portal
  2. Get DPIIT recognition certificate
  3. Apply for tax exemption with Inter-Ministerial Board
  4. Access Fund of Funds through registered venture funds
