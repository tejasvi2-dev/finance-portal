Mission & Vision

Guiding principles that drive India's financial governance

Our Purpose

Building India's Economic Foundation

Our Mission

To manage India's public finances responsibly, promote sustainable economic growth, and ensure financial inclusion for every citizen. We are committed to transparent governance and data-driven policy making.

Our Vision

To make India a $10 trillion economy by 2030 through innovation, sustainable development, digital transformation, and transparent governance across all sectors.

Foundation

Our Core Values

Transparency

Open governance with accessible data, public accountability, and proactive disclosure of financial information.

Accountability

Every rupee spent is accounted for through rigorous auditing, performance monitoring, and outcome-based budgeting.

Inclusivity

Policies designed to reach the last mile — ensuring financial services and opportunities are available to every citizen.

Innovation

Embracing technology and data-driven approaches to modernize financial governance and service delivery.

Fiscal Prudence

Maintaining fiscal discipline while investing strategically in growth drivers and social welfare programs.

Citizen-Centric

Every policy and service is designed keeping the citizen at the center — simplifying processes and reducing barriers.

Historic reforms opening India's economy to global markets.

World's largest financial inclusion program — 50Cr+ accounts.

One Nation, One Tax — India's biggest tax reform.
