Key Highlights

The Union Budget 2026-27, presented by the Hon. Finance Minister, marks a historic milestone with a total outlay of ₹48.13 Lakh Crore. The budget prioritizes capital expenditure, social welfare, and India's transition to a digital economy.

Major Allocations
  • Infrastructure: ₹11,11,111 Crore — Roads, railways, ports, airports
  • Defence: ₹6,21,540 Crore — National security and modernization
  • Agriculture: ₹1,25,036 Crore — Kisan support and rural development
  • Education: ₹1,20,628 Crore — NEP implementation and skill development
  • Healthcare: ₹89,155 Crore — Ayushman Bharat and hospital upgrades
  • Technology: ₹16,549 Crore — Digital India 3.0 and AI initiatives
Tax Reforms

The new tax regime now offers zero tax for income up to ₹7.5 Lakh, simplified ITR filing, and enhanced deductions for housing, education, and health insurance. Corporate tax for MSMEs reduced to 15%.

Digital Economy Push

₹10,000 Crore allocated for AI and emerging technologies. UPI infrastructure expansion, digital rupee pilot expansion, and cybersecurity framework strengthening are key focus areas.
