Building India's Economic Foundation

The Ministry of Finance is the apex body responsible for administration of the finances of the Government of India. It is concerned with taxation, financial legislation, financial institutions, capital markets, centre and state finances, and the Union Budget.

With a commitment to transparency, fiscal discipline, and inclusive growth, the department drives policies that impact the lives of over 1.4 billion citizens.

Key Departments

The Ministry of Finance comprises five key departments

Department of Economic Affairs

Formulates economic policy and manages public debt and international financial relations.

Department of Expenditure

Oversees public expenditure management and financial rules of the Government of India.

Department of Revenue

Administers direct and indirect tax policies including Income Tax, GST and Customs.

Dept. of Investment & Public Asset Mgmt

Manages government investments in public sector enterprises and disinvestment strategies.

Dept. of Financial Services

Oversees banking, insurance, and pension sectors ensuring financial stability.

Central Board of Direct Taxes

Responsible for administration of direct tax laws including income tax and corporate tax.
